Permits & Process

Working with the
City of Brantford

Renovations that involve new framing, added electrical circuits, plumbing, or a secondary suite generally require a permit from the City of Brantford Building Division. We submit the application, manage inspections, and schedule around approval timelines — the biggest variable on most Brantford projects — so that process doesn't fall on you.

Common Questions

Brantford FAQs

Do you handle permits for renovations in Brantford?+

Yes. For projects requiring permits — additions, structural work, ARUs and finished basements with new egress — we submit the application to the City of Brantford Building Division and manage the inspection process. You don't need to navigate the city's building department yourself.

What kinds of Brantford homes have you worked on?+

A wide range — post-war bungalows in neighbourhoods like Holmedale and Dufferin, and newer infill and builds in areas like Wyndfield and West Brant. Older Brantford homes often surface knob-and-tube wiring, cast-iron drain stacks or asbestos-containing floor tile once we open the walls, and we price a realistic contingency for that upfront.

Can I add a secondary suite (ARU) to my Brantford home?+

In many cases, yes. The City of Brantford has expanded zoning to allow accessory dwelling units in more areas, but lot coverage, parking and zoning permissions vary by neighbourhood. We handle the approvals and know where the friction points typically are.
