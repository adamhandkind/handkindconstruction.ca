Case-by-Case in Cambridge

Honest about
distance and fit

HandKind is based in Paris, Ontario, with Brantford, Paris and Brant County as our core service area. Cambridge sits roughly 30-40 minutes away, so we take on Cambridge projects selectively rather than advertising the same availability we have closer to home. When the scope and schedule line up, we run the same managed process — detailed estimate, permits, trade co-ordination and site management — that we run everywhere else.

Cambridge itself is a city of distinct older cores — Galt, Preston and Hespeler — amalgamated in 1973, each with its own mix of century homes, post-war housing and newer builds. If you're planning a renovation, tell us about your home's age and layout during the estimate call and we'll flag anything specific to plan around.

Common Questions

Cambridge FAQs

Do you take on renovation projects in Cambridge?+

Yes, on a case-by-case basis. HandKind is based in Paris, Ontario, roughly 30-40 minutes from Cambridge, and our primary service area is Brantford, Paris and Brant County. Yes. Cambridge renovation and addition permits are issued by the City of Cambridge Building Division. We manage the permit application and inspection process the same way we do for our primary service area.

Does the extra distance to Cambridge affect scheduling?+

It's a factor we're upfront about.
