What's Included

kitchen renovation

01

Demolition & Rough Work

We remove existing cabinetry, flooring and finishes carefully, preserving anything worth keeping. Subfloor issues, hidden water damage and outdated wiring are flagged and resolved at this stage before they can derail the schedule later.

02

Layout & Wall Removal

Many Brantford homes built between the 1970s and 1990s have closed-off kitchens that don't suit modern family life. We assess load-bearing structures, arrange engineering where required, and open up the space properly — beams, columns and all.

03

Cabinetry & Millwork

We work with both custom and semi-custom cabinetry and handle all installation in-house. Crown moulding, island framing, pantry build-outs and integrated appliance surrounds are part of what we build, not a separate line item.

04

Countertops & Tile

Quartz, stone and porcelain surfaces are templated and installed as part of the package. We handle backsplash tile, floor tile and transitions to adjoining rooms without subbing the work to a separate crew that doesn't know your project.

05

Lighting Design

Lighting is one of the most overlooked parts of a kitchen renovation. We plan task lighting, under-cabinet strips and pendant placement before the rough-in so the electrical is done correctly the first time — not patched after the drywall is up.

06

Plumbing Co-ordination

Sink relocations, dishwasher rough-ins and pot-filler lines are co-ordinated with our plumbing partners. Trades are scheduled to arrive when the site is ready — not called in on short notice and left to sort out what the previous crew left behind.

A kitchen renovation in Brantford costs $25,000–$80,000+. A cabinet and countertop refresh with minimal layout changes starts around $25,000–$35,000. A full gut renovation with layout changes, new cabinetry, tile, lighting and plumbing updates typically runs $50,000–$80,000+. HandKind provides detailed line-item quotes with no vague allowances — you know exactly what's included before work starts.

How long does a kitchen renovation take in Brantford?

Most kitchen renovations in Brantford take 4–8 weeks from demolition to final handover, depending on scope and material lead times. Projects involving structural wall removal or significant plumbing and electrical changes typically fall on the longer end. We provide a week-by-week schedule before work starts and update you at each stage so you always know where the project stands.

Do I need a permit for a kitchen renovation in Brantford?

Most cosmetic kitchen renovations in Brantford do not require a permit — cabinet replacements, countertops, tile and painting all fall into this category. Permits are required when the renovation involves structural changes (such as removing a load-bearing wall), relocating plumbing stacks, or upgrading the electrical panel. HandKind handles all permit applications as part of the project scope when required — you don't need to deal with the city yourself.
