Six Pillars

What we do
differently

01

Craftsmanship
over speed

We don't race through work to free up the crew for the next project. Real craftsmanship takes the time it takes — and corners that get cut are corners you live with for years.

  • Straight lines, tight reveals and clean details throughout
  • Framing done right before a single finish material goes in
  • Finish carpentry and millwork that fits — not forced
  • We hold our work to a standard we'd accept in our own homes
02

Transparent budgets,
zero surprises

Renovation horror stories almost always start with a vague quote and an allowance that turned into a much bigger number. We quote in line-item detail before work begins — so you know exactly what's included.

  • Detailed, itemised scope of work before signing
  • No hidden allowances or fuzzy "TBDs"
  • Every change order documented and approved before work proceeds
  • Purchase orders tracked so costs don't drift
03

Full project
management

You hired a contractor — not a project management job. We coordinate all trades, track lead times on materials, manage site inspections and keep the schedule moving. One call, one contact, one point of accountability.

  • All subtrades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC) coordinated by us
  • Material selections guided with lead times tracked
  • Permit applications and inspection scheduling handled
  • Regular site updates so you're never left wondering
04

Renovation
specialists

Renovations and new builds are fundamentally different work. We don't take on new construction on the side. Kitchens, bathrooms, basements, additions and ARUs are what we do every single day — and the depth shows.

  • Specialists in what happens inside occupied homes
  • Deep experience navigating existing structure and mechanicals
  • We know where renovations go wrong — and how to prevent it
  • No general contracting, no handyman side jobs
05

Owner-involved
on every project

We're not a franchise. We're not a large company that hands you off to a site supervisor you've never met. Jason is on every project — and his name is on the company, so the accountability is personal.

  • Not a franchise model or national brand
  • The person who sells you the work is the person who builds it
  • Small, consistent crew on every project
  • Owner reachable throughout — not just at the estimate
06

Local and
long-term

We live and work in Brant County. We expect to run into our clients at the store, on the trail, and at school events for years to come. That's not a sales line — it's the most honest form of accountability there is.

  • Based in Paris, serving Brantford and Brant County
  • We answer after handover — the relationship doesn't end
  • Warranty support on workmanship
  • Referral-driven — most of our work comes from past clients
