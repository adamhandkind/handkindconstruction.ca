Gut vs. Refresh

A bathroom renovation in Brantford can mean very different things depending on what you're starting with. Sometimes a dated bathroom needs a cosmetic refresh — a new vanity, updated fixtures, fresh tile over a sound subfloor — and that's entirely appropriate if the structure beneath is solid.

Often, though, a bathroom that looks tired on the surface has deeper problems underneath: water damage behind the tile, a failed shower pan, subfloor rot from years of slow leaks, or drywall installed directly in a wet environment with no waterproofing membrane behind it. Those problems don't go away with a new vanity top.

HandKind handles full bathroom renovations in Brantford, Paris and across Brant County. We assess what's actually there before we write a scope, so you're not quoted for a refresh and then surprised mid-project when we find rot behind the shower wall. Our estimates are based on reality, not best-case assumptions.

Our Standard

Waterproofing is where
we don't compromise

The majority of bathroom failures we see when we gut an older space come down to one thing: inadequate waterproofing. Tile was set directly on drywall. A shower pan was hot-mopped once and never properly sloped. Cement board was installed but joints were never taped or sealed. The tile looks fine for a few years, then the framing quietly saturates, and what looked like a $15,000 renovation turns into a $40,000 structural repair.

For any bathroom renovation in Brantford we take on, Schluter systems are our baseline — not an upgrade. KERDI on every shower wall, DITRA under every tiled floor. These aren't upsells. They're the minimum standard for work we're willing to put our name on and stand behind when a homeowner calls us five years later.

  • Schluter KERDI waterproof membrane on all shower walls
  • Schluter DITRA uncoupling membrane on tiled floors
  • Pre-sloped shower bases — no flat pans
  • Cement board or Schluter KERDI-BOARD substrate throughout
  • All penetrations and corners fully sealed before tile

Local Knowledge

What we know about older
Brantford & Paris bathrooms

The bulk of our bathroom renovation work in Brantford and Paris happens in homes built between the 1950s and 1980s — and these homes have a very specific set of characteristics that newer-build renovations simply don't present.

Cast iron tubs are one of the first things we assess. They're heavy, but they're also often in excellent structural condition after 60 years. If the enamel is sound and the homeowner wants to keep it, we can work around it. If the tub is staying, the surrounding tile work still needs to be stripped and done correctly — a new surround installed over old greenboard is just deferring the same problem.

The 1960s tile layouts we find most often in Brantford — 4×4 ceramic tile set directly on drywall with no waterproofing behind it, mosaic tile floors over bare concrete — were standard practice at the time. They've lasted 50+ years in some cases, but once water gets behind them, the damage compounds quickly. We find it when we demo, document it, and address it properly before the new substrate goes in.

Paris, Ontario homes add another variable: tighter bathroom footprints in older heritage builds, original cast-iron drain stacks that need adapters, and occasional knob-and-tube wiring that has to be dealt with before we can rough-in a new bathroom circuit. These aren't dealbreakers — they're just things an experienced Brantford bathroom renovation contractor knows to look for before the scope is written.

What does a bathroom renovation cost in Brantford?

A bathroom renovation in Brantford costs $15,000–$50,000+. A cosmetic refresh — new vanity, toilet, fixtures and paint — starts around $8,000–$15,000. A full gut renovation with a tiled shower, in-floor heating, new plumbing rough-in and custom tile typically runs $20,000–$35,000. A full ensuite with a freestanding tub, custom glass shower and high-end finishes starts at $35,000 and up. HandKind provides detailed line-item quotes before any work begins — no vague allowances.

How long does a bathroom renovation take in Brantford?

A standard bathroom renovation in Brantford takes 3–5 weeks. A main bathroom with no layout changes typically runs 2–3 weeks. A full ensuite with custom tile, a glass enclosure and in-floor heating takes 4–6 weeks. Material lead times — especially tile, vanities and glass — are the biggest variable, and we track them before work starts so they don't delay your project mid-build.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom renovation in Brantford?

Most bathroom renovations in Brantford do not require a permit — including full gut-and-rebuild projects that replace tile, fixtures and a vanity in the same footprint. A permit is required if the renovation adds a new bathroom that didn't previously exist, relocates plumbing to a new location, or involves structural changes. HandKind advises on permit requirements during the estimate and handles all applications when needed.
