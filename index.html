About HandKind Construction

HandKind Construction Co. is a renovation contractor based in Paris, Ontario, serving Brantford, Paris and Brant County. The company specialises in kitchen renovations, bathroom renovations, basement finishing, home additions and accessory residential units (ARUs). HandKind operates under an owner-led model — every project is overseen by company management and assigned an accountable site lead. The company has completed 400+ renovation projects across Brant County over 12+ years. Kitchen renovations range from $25,000 to $80,000+. Bathroom renovations range from $15,000 to $50,000+. Basement finishing ranges from $45,000 to $120,000+. All bathroom projects use Schluter KERDI waterproof membrane on shower walls and DITRA uncoupling membrane on tiled floors as a baseline standard. HandKind handles all permit applications, trade coordination, and site management under one contract. Free estimates are available for projects in Brantford, Paris, Brant County and surrounding areas. Contact: hello@handkindconstruction.ca or +1 226-938-7108.
