Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear often.

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Vancouver? +

Most kitchen renovations in Vancouver range from $40,000 to $120,000+ depending on scope, size, and finish level. A mid-range kitchen with new cabinetry, countertops, appliances, and tile typically falls between $55,000–$85,000. We provide fixed-price quotes so there are no surprises.

Do you handle permits? +

Yes — permit management is included in our service for all projects that require one. We prepare the drawings, submit the applications, and coordinate all city inspections from start to finish.

How long does a typical renovation take? +

A bathroom renovation typically takes 2–4 weeks. A kitchen renovation is 4–8 weeks. A basement suite or larger addition can take 3–6 months. We provide a detailed schedule before work begins and update you at each phase.

Can I live in my home during the renovation? +

For most kitchen and bathroom renovations, yes — many clients remain in their homes throughout. For larger gut renovations or basement suites requiring significant structural work, we'll discuss the best approach during consultation.

Are you licensed and insured in BC? +

Yes. Handkind Construction holds a valid BC Contractor licence, carries $5M general liability insurance, and maintains WCB coverage for all workers on site. We're happy to provide documentation on request.

Do you offer a warranty on your work? +

All our projects include a 2-year workmanship warranty in addition to applicable BC New Home Warranty protections for qualifying projects. We stand behind our work long after the final walkthrough.
